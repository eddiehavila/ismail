Tambopata: credits

The song contains none of the audio below. ismail measured each recording (a call's pitch contour and harmonics, an instrument's sound, a player's timing and phrasing) and the song rebuilds it with synthesis. These recordings and these players are where every sound in it comes from.

The players across the river

The village drum and the dancers' seed rattles play the timing of a Matsigenka kypari dance, measured from Danza matsigenka kypari, jóvenes rescatando la cultura, recorded by the Dirección de Patrimonio Inmaterial, Ministerio de Cultura del Perú.

The panpipe's phrasing is measured from a sonkari played in the Asháninka native community of San Miguel, SONKARI: instrumento tradicional amazónico Asháninka, filmed by Paolo Cogliati.
